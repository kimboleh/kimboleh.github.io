Normally in a space like this, I'd stumble all over myself typing up some weak Hi, welcome to my website, my name's [sdfldjs] and I'm an [age] [gender] in [location] - but this is the Indie Web, gosh darnit, and I'm going to use this space to be as goofy and cringe and free as I was before capitalism and neurotypical-normativity crushed it out of me!!

Ahem. So with that out of the way -

Hi. Good to see you here, and welcome to my website.

Like many of us, I was once a small lass on Web 1.0, creating webpages in Notepad (I had no idea how to buy hosting space, so all my HTML/CSS sites lived locally on my PC!) and dreaming one day of becoming an author. I actually grew up to become a web developer, which I think young me would still find pretty cool, but I keenly miss the days where I could type for hours about whatever interested me, and sometimes that would make it onto a Blogspot blog, and my aunt would read every word and tell me she loved it.

The good news: those days can exist again. It's no Blogspot, but this is my space to type away about whatever I'm thinking about in the moment, from HTML to video essays to Cuba.

If you're interested in what you see in my sidebar, or you like reading strangers' writing, or you just like exploring the indie web, I hope you enjoy your time here! Please feel free to leave a comment in my guestbook to let me know you were here. :3
